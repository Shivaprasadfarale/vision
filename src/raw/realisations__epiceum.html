Epiceum

Relocation to a New Office Floor

Sector Media & Communications
Location Montreuil
Duration 2 Months

A Project Co-Created with All Epiceum Employees

Epiceum aimed to relocate to a new office floor to create a more welcoming and functional workspace for all employees. We had the privilege of supporting Epiceum throughout this important transition, providing guidance at every stage of the change process.

The project was carried out in close collaboration with employees through a participatory approach, gathering their feedback and suggestions. The goal was to create a space that truly reflects the team’s identity and needs.

Particular attention was given to incorporating greenery into the new offices. By integrating plants and green areas, we aimed to create a more pleasant and inspiring work environment that enhances employee well-being and motivation.

"Designing a collaborative workspace tailored to our activity proved to be essential, both for the efficiency of our operations and the well-being of our teams. Thank you for this wonderful project, which we enjoy every day! All our visitors are envious!"
Christian De La Guéronnière Chief Executive Officer – Epiceum

The Importance of a Work Café in Strengthening Team Cohesion

At Epiceum, the creation of a work café was essential in bringing employees together. Designed as both a relaxation area and a place for informal interaction, it provides a welcoming environment where colleagues can take a break, enjoy a hot drink, and connect in a friendly and inspiring atmosphere.

This space supports not only individual well-being but also stronger team cohesion. In addition, the terrace plays a key role in enhancing the employee experience, offering an enjoyable outdoor space where people can recharge and step away from their daily tasks.

This balanced environment helps reduce stress, strengthen employee engagement, and improve productivity, while creating a more enjoyable and fulfilling workplace.
