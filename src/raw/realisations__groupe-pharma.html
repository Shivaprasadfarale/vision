Groupe Pharmaceutique

European Headquarters Fit-Out

Sector Healthcare & Life Sciences
Location Grand Paris
Duration 3 Months
Expertise Consulting · Design · Project Delivery

Collaboration and Connection Above the Paris Skyline

An International Pharmaceutical Group recently relocated to a new space at a different address to establish its European headquarters.

This redesign aims to create a welcoming environment that fosters collaboration for visiting employees. Our goal was to design an attractive workplace where teams feel motivated to come together, collaborate, and exchange ideas every day.

The new space, filled with natural light, features ergonomic workstations, a convivial relaxation area, and multiple meeting rooms.

The meeting rooms were designed with an original and inspiring theme, evoking a stroll across the rooftops of Paris, providing an ideal setting to stimulate creativity and interaction.

"Employees were eager to move into the new spaces, and they were absolutely amazed by the final result, which perfectly matched our expectations. A successfully completed challenge."
Confidential Office manager, Groupe Pharmaceutique
