Workspace Redesign with a View

As part of a comprehensive transformation of its workplace, this entire floor was completely redesigned to meet the requirements of a flex office. Located at a prestigious address with direct views of the Arc de Triomphe, the project combines functionality with an exceptional setting.

The layout includes standardized workstations, all equipped with uniform connectivity, as well as individual locker systems allowing each employee to settle in freely. The entire floor was designed to streamline workflows and encourage internal mobility.

A coworking and client reception area, taking full advantage of the panoramic views, completes the setup—blending everyday practicality with a polished image for visitors.
